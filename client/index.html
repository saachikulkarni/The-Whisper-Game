Sit in a Circle. Whisper Secrets. Break Logic!

Players sit in a clockwise circle. Pass secret words clockwise (1 ➔ 2 ➔ ... ➔ N ➔ 1). Then ask hilarious questions anticlockwise (N ➔ N-1 ➔ ... ➔ 1). Reveal your secret word and vote if it makes perfect sense!
